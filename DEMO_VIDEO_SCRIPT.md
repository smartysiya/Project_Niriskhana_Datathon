## 🎙️ Video Recording Tips for the Team

1. **Screen Resolution**: Record at **1080p (1920x1080)** in Fullscreen mode (`F11`).
2. **Browser Cursor**: Use a clean mouse cursor with highlight clicks enabled (e.g. OBS Studio / Camtasia).
3. **Audio Quality**: Record voiceover with a clear USB microphone or generate high-quality AI voiceover (e.g., ElevenLabs / Murf.ai).
4. **Speed & Pacing**: Keep transitions smooth. Scroll naturally without sudden jerks.
